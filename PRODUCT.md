## Platform

## Stack

Next.js + Vercel (user's choice, 2026-10-07). Prototype of the home page only; existing pages and URLs of dv.by stay as they are and are linked to.

## What it is

Home page prototype for «Доктор Вет» — a network of two 24/7 veterinary clinics in Minsk (Подшипниковый пр., 9 and ул. Скрипникова, 39), working since 2006. Built as a cold-lead demo by Nikita Chystsiakov to show the clinic owner a better home page.

## Users and their scene

- Pet owners in Minsk (mostly dogs and cats), choosing or returning to a clinic from a phone. Some are calm (vaccination, check-up), some anxious (the animal is ill, sometimes at night).
- The clinic owner/manager who opens the demo link, likely on a phone, and must recognise their own clinic within seconds.

## Jobs

1. Understand that this is a strong clinic (ICU, lab, MRI, 100+ specialists) — trust.
2. Book an appointment: pick a service and/or a doctor and/or a date, optionally a clinic; see real free times; leave name, phone, pet type. Booking is a request that an administrator confirms by phone.
3. In an emergency: call or go now. 21:00–9:00 there is no booking — a duty vet sees patients in a live queue with a night tariff.
4. Find services, prices, doctors, clinic addresses (links to existing dv.by pages).

## Brand commitments

- Keep the existing logo (red-crimson cross with paw #C6204A + «Доктор Вет» in blue #0E76BB). Owner must recognise their brand.
- Real photography only: their doctor portraits (uniform blue-grey backdrop), clinic facades and interiors. No stock pets presented as theirs.
- No invented claims, prices, awards or reviews. Demo slot data is synthetic and labelled.

## User (designer) constraints from feedback

- Must not read as AI-generated: rejected — headline left + photo right + two buttons + row of four stat boxes; rounded cards and pill chips; uppercase kicker above the headline; blue+grey, navy+crimson and cream palettes.
- Booking must exist and be easy but must not own the whole first screen; the first screen sells the clinic's strength.
- Booking form: three interdependent dropdowns — service, doctor, date — plus clinic filter «Любая / Подшипниковый / Скрипникова». Choosing a service narrows doctors; choosing a doctor narrows services; dates show only days with free slots. Choosing from a list, not typing.
- Night mode after 21:00: duty vet, no booking needed; slots for the morning.
- All phone numbers listed (footer + call sheet).
- Light first screen (medicine).

## Accessibility

Russian-language, mobile-first; large tap targets; readable for older owners.
